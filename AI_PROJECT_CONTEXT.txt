1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & NON-DIAGNOSTIC SAFETY BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VoxNeuro is a local, privacy-preserving acoustic voice analysis and neurology
research platform prototype designed to investigate acoustic biomarkers in sustained
vowel recordings (/a/).

CRITICAL RESEARCH & SAFETY BOUNDARY:
- VoxNeuro is an exploratory engineering prototype and research software platform.
- It is EXPLICITLY NON-DIAGNOSTIC. It never issues a clinical diagnosis, probability
  of disease, or clinical rule-out for any live audio or user microphone recording.
- Ingestion of live microphone audio is gated by `can_predict_recording_domain()`,
  which immediately rejects patient-level inference. This fail-closed safety gate
  prevents misapplication of retrospective research models trained on telephone
  recordings (UAMS cohort) to uncalibrated laptop or smartphone microphones.
- Standalone HTML reports, JSON exports, and Streamlit UI panels prominently display
  clinical disclaimers and omit diagnostic inferences.

3. ACOUSTIC FEATURE EXTRACTION PIPELINE (CORE39 SCHEMA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VoxNeuro implements the `voxneuro.voice.v3` schema, extracting exactly 39 clinically
grounded acoustic features from sustained /a/ phonations:

1. Pitch & Fundamental Frequency (F0):
   - `f0_mean_hz`, `f0_sd_hz`, `f0_min_hz`, `f0_max_hz`
   - Extracted using Praat's accurate auto-correlation algorithm via Parselmouth.

2. Perturbation & Instability Measures:
   - Jitter: `jitter_local_percent`, `jitter_absolute_us`, `jitter_rap_percent`,
     `jitter_ppq5_percent`, `jitter_ddp_percent`
   - Shimmer: `shimmer_local_percent`, `shimmer_local_db`, `shimmer_apq3_percent`,
     `shimmer_apq5_percent`, `shimmer_apq11_percent`, `shimmer_dda_percent`

3. Harmonicity & Noise:
   - Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio: `hnr_mean_db`, `hnr_sd_db`
     * NOTE: Praat marks unvoiced frames with -200 dB. VoxNeuro masks these frames
       (hnr > -199 dB) before computing statistics, ensuring scientific accuracy.
   - Noise-to-Harmonics Ratio: `nhr_mean`
   - Autocorrelation: `autocorrelation_mean`, `autocorrelation_sd`

4. Spectral Distribution & Timbre:
   - `spectral_centroid_hz`, `spectral_bandwidth_hz`, `spectral_rolloff_hz` (85%)
   - `spectral_flatness`, `spectral_contrast_mean_db`, `spectral_contrast_sd_db`

5. Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC 0 to 12):
   - `mfcc_0_mean` through `mfcc_12_mean` (13 cepstral mean coefficients)
   - `mfcc_0_sd` through `mfcc_12_sd` (13 cepstral standard deviations)

All extraction steps enforce strict preconditions: samples must be finite, real
numeric float arrays in [-1.0, 1.0]; sample rates must be integers in 8000..48000 Hz;
and Nyquist boundaries are strictly validated.

4. MACHINE LEARNING, DEMOGRAPHIC CONFOUNDING & EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Retrospective Dataset: UAMS voice cohort (telephone recordings, 8000 Hz, 78 subjects:
  37 Parkinson's Disease [PD] and 41 Healthy Controls [HC]).
- Strict Leakage Prevention:
  * All evaluations enforce GroupKFold on `participant_id`. No participant's recordings
    span across train and test folds.
  * Repeated nested cross-validation (5 outer folds x 3 inner tuning folds x 3 repeats).
- Demographic Confounding Analysis:
  * In the UAMS cohort, PD participants were significantly older than HC participants
    (PD Mean Age: 67.04 ± 8.98 yrs vs HC Mean Age: 47.73 ± 14.33 yrs; Δ = 19.3 yrs).
  * An age-only logistic baseline achieves 83.9% Balanced Accuracy, outperforming
    the top voice-only model (Extra Trees: 76.2% ± 2.0% BA, 0.784 ROC-AUC, 95% CI 0.664–0.850).
  * VoxNeuro prominently surfaces this confounding finding to alert researchers that
    acoustic discrimination without age-matched controls must not be mistaken for
    disease specificity.
- Fail-Closed Receipt Validation:
  * The Streamlit UI verifies the nested evaluation receipt, code version hash,
    and artifact SHA-256 digests BEFORE displaying findings, ensuring fail-closed integrity.

5. FRONTEND & 3D THREE.JS ACOUSTIC VISUALIZER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Located at `frontend/acoustic-visualizer/`.
- Built with Three.js (r160) and bundled via esbuild into `dist/visualizer.bundle.js`.
- Waveform 3D:
  * Renders a sculpted 3D volumetric ribbon with local-energy depth extrusion in Z.
  * Dynamic color grading from mineral blue to warm stone and burnt vermilion.
  * Illuminated with hemisphere and directional lighting, plus a glowing crest line.
- Spectrogram 3D:
  * Renders short-time Fourier transform magnitude (-90 dB to 0 dB full scale).
  * Smooth thermographic palette: midnight slate -> mineral blue -> warm amber -> burnt vermilion.
  * Interactive Raycaster Hover Probe: Real-time numeric HUD readout indicating
    Time (s), Frequency (Hz), and Magnitude (dB) under mouse cursor with a 3D marker.
  * Camera Presets: One-click buttons for [3D Isometric], [Front Spectrum],
    [Top-Down Spectrogram], and [Reset].
- Resilient Fallback: Automatic fallback to an accessible 2D HTML5 Canvas rendering
  if WebGL2 is interrupted or unavailable.
