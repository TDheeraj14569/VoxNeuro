1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & NON-DIAGNOSTIC SAFETY BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VoxNeuro is a local, privacy-preserving acoustic voice analysis and neurology research
platform prototype engineered to investigate literature-informed acoustic measurements
in sustained vowel phonations (/a/).

CRITICAL RESEARCH & SAFETY BOUNDARY:
- VoxNeuro is an exploratory engineering prototype and research software platform.
- It is EXPLICITLY NON-DIAGNOSTIC. It never issues a clinical diagnosis, probability
  of disease, or clinical rule-out for any live audio or user microphone recording.
- Ingestion of live microphone audio is gated by `can_predict_recording_domain()`,
  which immediately rejects patient-level inference. This fail-closed safety gate
  prevents misapplication of retrospective research models trained on telephone
  recordings (UAMS cohort) to uncalibrated laptop or smartphone microphones.
- Standalone HTML reports, JSON telemetry exports, and Streamlit UI panels prominently
  display clinical disclaimers and omit diagnostic inferences.
- Audio samples are held transiently in memory; zero raw audio or patient identifiers
  are stored to disk by default.

3. ACOUSTIC FEATURE EXTRACTION PIPELINE (EXACT CORE39 SCHEMA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VoxNeuro implements the `voxneuro.voice.v3` schema, extracting exactly 39
literature-informed acoustic measurements from sustained /a/ phonations:

1. Pitch & Fundamental Frequency (F0) [6 Features]:
   - `f0_mean_hz`: Mean voiced fundamental frequency across valid frames (Hz).
   - `f0_median_hz`: Median voiced fundamental frequency across valid frames (Hz).
   - `f0_sd_hz`: Sample standard deviation of voiced fundamental frequency (Hz).
   - `f0_min_hz`: Minimum observed voiced fundamental frequency (Hz).
   - `f0_max_hz`: Maximum observed voiced fundamental frequency (Hz).
   - `f0_range_hz`: Pitch range (`f0_max_hz - f0_min_hz`) (Hz).
   * Extracted using Praat's autocorrelation pitch tracker via Parselmouth.

2. Voice Perturbation & Instability Measures [3 Features]:
   - `jitter_local`: Praat local jitter; cycle-to-cycle pitch period variation (ratio).
   - `shimmer_local`: Praat local shimmer; cycle-to-cycle peak amplitude variation (ratio).
   - `hnr_mean_db`: Mean harmonics-to-noise ratio of valid voiced frames (dB).
     * SCIENTIFIC NOTE: Praat marks unvoiced frames with -200 dB. VoxNeuro strictly
       masks these undefined frames (`hnr > -199 dB`) before computing statistics,
       ensuring scientific accuracy.

3. Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC 00 to 12) [26 Features]:
   - `mfcc_00_mean` through `mfcc_12_mean` (13 cepstral mean coefficients).
   - `mfcc_00_sd` through `mfcc_12_sd` (13 cepstral standard deviations).
   * Extracted via Librosa from the 40-band log-Mel power spectrum.

4. Spectral Distribution & Temporal Dynamics [4 Features]:
   - `spectral_centroid_mean_hz`: Mean spectral center of mass (Hz).
   - `spectral_rolloff_mean_hz`: Mean 85% energy rolloff frequency (Hz).
   - `spectral_bandwidth_mean_hz`: Mean second-order spectral spread (Hz).
   - `zero_crossing_rate_mean`: Mean rate of sign-changes across frames (fraction).

Exact Schema Sum: 6 (Pitch) + 3 (Voice) + 26 (MFCC) + 4 (Spectral) = Exactly 39 Features.

Preconditions & Signal Contracts:
- Audio samples must form a 1D monophonic float array normalized strictly to [-1.0, 1.0].
- Values must be finite with no NaN or infinite entries.
- Sample rates must be integers in 8,000..48,000 Hz.
- Parameter boundaries (`pitch_floor_hz`, `pitch_ceiling_hz`, FFT windows) are validated.

4. MACHINE LEARNING, DEMOGRAPHIC CONFOUNDING & EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Retrospective Dataset: UAMS voice cohort (telephone recordings, 8000 Hz, 78 subjects:
  37 Parkinson's Disease [PD] and 41 Healthy Controls [HC]).
- Participant-Disjoint Grouping:
  * All evaluations enforce `StratifiedGroupKFold` on `participant_id`. No participant's
    recordings span across train and test folds.
  * Repeated nested cross-validation: 5 outer folds x 3 inner tuning folds x 3 repeat seeds.
- Explicit Demographic Confounding Analysis:
  * In the UAMS cohort, PD participants were significantly older than HC participants
    (PD Mean Age: 67.04 ± 8.76 yrs vs HC Mean Age: 47.73 ± 13.56 yrs; Δ = 19.31 yrs).
  * An age-only logistic baseline achieves 83.9% ± 0.7% Balanced Accuracy, outperforming
    the top voice-only model (Extra Trees: 76.2% ± 2.0% BA, 0.784 ROC-AUC, 95% CI 0.664–0.850).
  * VoxNeuro prominently surfaces this confounding finding to alert researchers that
    acoustic discrimination without age-matched controls must not be mistaken for
    disease specificity.
- Fail-Closed Receipt Validation:
  * The Streamlit UI verifies the nested evaluation receipt, code version hash,
    and artifact SHA-256 digests BEFORE displaying findings, ensuring fail-closed integrity.
