# Limitations

- This prototype is not clinically validated, not a diagnosis, and not authorized for medical use.
- Voice abnormalities are non-specific and may reflect age, respiratory/laryngeal conditions, other neurologic or speech conditions, medication, fatigue, language, environment, device response, or recording quality.
- The UAMS public cohort is small, telephone-collected, and potentially demographically confounded. Local manifest and audit results must accompany any metrics.
- The UCI dataset provides repeated pre-extracted features and is a separate research benchmark, not raw audio validation.
- Internal participant-grouped cross-validation is not an external test. It does not establish transportability to India, primary care, different languages, or browser microphones.
- F0/jitter/shimmer/HNR and spectral summaries vary with task, source Nyquist, settings, device response, and environment. Amplitude is not calibrated loudness.
- `/a/` phonation contains no direct articulation, connected-speech rhythm, speech rate, intelligibility, or spontaneous-speech information.
- Streamlit browser microphone capture has not been demonstrated on all browsers/devices. Permission, secure context, and browser audio processing can alter capture.
- No clinician interviews, patient recruitment, ethics review, technical repeatability study, prospective validation, or regulatory consultation has occurred.
