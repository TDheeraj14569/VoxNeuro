# Research gap and project boundary

The literature supports studying speech and voice features associated with PD, but study-to-study variation in cohorts, tasks, devices, preprocessing, and validation limits transfer. The UAMS dataset has only 81 phone-collected sustained vowels, while UCI is an unrelated feature-only cohort with repeated recordings and many derived columns. Neither alone establishes validity for fresh laptop or smartphone recordings in India or primary care.

The prototype gap is technical and methodological: implement a transparent local acquisition/quality pipeline and make data lineage, feature definitions, participant-wise evaluation, and confounding checks reproducible. A clinical need and product gap still require local primary research. Device-domain compatibility and external clinical performance remain unproven.
