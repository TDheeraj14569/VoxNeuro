# Existing approaches and solutions

**Status: LITERATURE EVIDENCE, not a market or procurement audit.**

- Clinician neurological examination and referral remain the reference pathway; speech-language pathologists assess communication and dysarthria using perceptual, functional, and acoustic methods.
- Research measures include sustained phonation, sentence reading, diadochokinesis, and connected speech; feature sets include F0/prosody, jitter, shimmer, HNR/CPP, MFCCs, timing, articulation, and task-specific measures.
- Telemonitoring studies have explored repeated speech tasks for symptom tracking; evidence from longitudinal monitoring of known patients does not prove general-population screening performance.
- Digital speech platforms and commercial products have been described in reviews, but current comparative evidence and regulatory status vary by product and jurisdiction. No procurement, regulatory, or clinical-performance claim is made for any named vendor in this project.
- Potential approach categories: clinician-administered acoustic analysis; smartphone/browser capture; dedicated speech assessment software; remote rehabilitation platforms; local referral documentation. These need local stakeholder and competitive analysis.

**PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED:** confirm which tools are available and approved at intended sites, workflow integration, cost, language support, access, and whether outputs change decisions.
