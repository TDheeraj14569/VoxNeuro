# Clinical validation and pilot preparation

**Status: protocol preparation only. No institution, ethics approval, recruitment, patient data, or clinical pilot exists.**

## Intended future question

After a defined intended-use population and report role are agreed with clinical partners, evaluate whether a standardized voice-feature summary adds reproducible information to independent clinician assessment. Do not use an arbitrary small sample size: determine the sample size from the primary endpoint, target confidence-interval width, prevalence, expected attrition/failure, and external statistical/clinical input.

## Prospective design outline

- Multisite prospective cohort spanning intended settings and relevant differential conditions, with appropriate age/sex representation.
- Pre-register a protocol, primary endpoint, analysis plan, subgroup plan, and failure criteria before enrollment.
- Independent clinical reference assessment by qualified clinicians, blinded to model output where appropriate.
- Separate development data from a locked participant-level external test cohort and site/device evaluation.
- Preserve consent, pseudonymous IDs, diagnosis provenance, task/device/environment metadata, medication timing where justified, and data minimization.
- Report sensitivity, specificity, PPV/NPV at intended-use prevalence, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC where relevant, calibration, confidence intervals, recording failure, repeatability, subgroup/device variation, and adverse-use risks.
- Begin any pilot in silent mode; model results do not direct care. Advance only after ethics, institutional, regulatory, privacy, safety, usability, and statistical review.

## Required before collection

Institutional Ethics Committee determination/approval, site authorization, informed consent, recruitment materials, data-management plan, retention/deletion rules, access controls, incident response, protocol registration where applicable, and professional advice on applicable Indian law and medical-device rules.
