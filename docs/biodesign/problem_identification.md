## Observation → problem hypotheses

No local observations were supplied. Secondary literature suggests that voice and speech can be affected in PD and can be measured computationally, but it does not establish a local primary-care referral problem. The following are hypotheses to investigate, not reported problems:

1. Speech changes may be noticed by patients or family before a formal neurological assessment, but their significance may be uncertain.
2. Access to movement-disorder expertise and speech-language assessment may vary by setting.
3. Voice measures from remote devices may not be comparable across microphones, environments, or tasks.
4. Voice-only outputs may introduce false reassurance or unnecessary referrals if used outside a validated role.
