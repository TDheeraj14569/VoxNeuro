# Observation protocol

**Status: PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED. No observations have been conducted or represented here.**

## Purpose

Map the current recognition, assessment, documentation, referral, and follow-up of voice or speech changes that may accompany Parkinson's disease. The observer should test the need hypothesis, including evidence that voice analysis may not be the right solution.

## People–Process–Technology–Environment

- **People:** neurologists and movement-disorder specialists; primary-care doctors; speech-language pathologists; neurorehabilitation professionals; people living with PD; caregivers.
- **Process:** how symptoms are recognized; what differential assessment happens; how referrals are written, sent, received, and acted upon; how speech is assessed; how repeat visits compare voice; where patients are lost or delayed.
- **Technology:** clinical examination forms, voice measures, speech-language assessment, telehealth tools, referral records, existing digital platforms, and workarounds.
- **Environment:** hospital and neurology OPD; primary health centre; rehabilitation centre; home follow-up; telemedicine; rural and urban connectivity/access constraints.

## Method

Use non-participant observation only with site authorization, ethics determination where required, informed consent when applicable, and no collection of unnecessary identifiers. Start with a workflow map and a field-note template. Separate what was directly observed, what a stakeholder reported, and observer interpretation. Do not record audio or patient details for this discovery work unless separately approved.

## Questions to resolve

1. Where does uncertainty occur and for whom?
2. What information changes a referral or assessment decision?
3. Which steps depend on a speech specialist or specialist neurologist?
4. How are voice changes currently assessed and recorded?
5. Which barriers arise from access, language, task burden, or device use?
6. What harm could a false flag or false reassurance create?
7. What would make a proposed intervention unacceptable or unworkable?
