# Preliminary need statement

**Status: DESIGN ASSUMPTION; PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED.** The project owner's preliminary hypothesis is: “A way to support timely recognition and appropriate referral of adults with possible Parkinson's disease in primary-care settings with limited access to specialist neurological assessment.” This is not a finding about any local care setting.

## Need framing

- **Population:** adults with possible Parkinsonism or speech/voice change presenting in a care pathway that may have limited specialist access.
- **Need:** a dependable and acceptable way to support recognition and timely referral while preserving the clinician's independent assessment.
- **Context:** primary care and referral networks; the actual local workflow, delay, and specialist availability remain unknown.
- **Potential outcome:** better structured information at referral, not a voice-only diagnosis.

## Evidence and uncertainty

**LITERATURE EVIDENCE:** PD may affect speech and voice, and research has studied phonation and connected speech as remote measures. However, methods and datasets differ materially; published work does not establish that this prototype can screen a primary-care population. The UAMS dataset is a small, telephone-recorded sustained-vowel cohort and the UCI set is pre-extracted data from repeated phonations.

**PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED:** determine who first suspects PD locally, how speech changes are noticed and documented, how referrals are made, where waits or failed handoffs occur, and which patient groups are underserved. Interview neurologists, primary-care clinicians, speech-language pathologists, patients, caregivers, and neurorehabilitation professionals. Do not recruit or collect patient data without institutional requirements.

**DESIGN ASSUMPTION:** a brief, standardized, locally run acoustic summary could be considered as supplementary information if clinicians and patients find the task acceptable and later validation demonstrates safe performance in the intended population.
