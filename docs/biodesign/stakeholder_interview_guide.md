# Stakeholder interview guide

**Status: PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED.** No interviews or clinician feedback have occurred.

## Opening

Explain that the discussion concerns current assessment and referral workflow, not a pitch for an AI product. Ask permission to take notes; do not collect identifiable patient information. Clarify that participation is voluntary and is not clinical research unless the responsible institution determines otherwise.

## Core prompts

1. Please walk me through the last time a patient arrived with new or changing speech or voice symptoms.
2. What happened from first concern through assessment, referral, and follow-up?
3. Which people or services were involved, and what information did each need?
4. At what points did the process slow down, repeat work, or lose information?
5. How are speech and voice changes distinguished from age, respiratory, laryngeal, medication, hearing, language, or other neurological factors?
6. What is documented today, and how are changes over time compared?
7. What happens when a recording or examination cannot be completed?
8. Which groups face the greatest barriers, and what evidence supports that view?
9. What would a false alarm or false reassurance mean in this workflow?
10. What information would make a referral more useful, and who should interpret it?
11. What local privacy, consent, infrastructure, and workload constraints apply?
12. What could make this type of supplementary tool inappropriate?

## Role-specific probes

- **Neurology:** differential diagnosis, referral thresholds, exam context, decision consequences.
- **Primary care:** recognition, escalation pathway, referral completion, time constraints.
- **Speech-language pathology:** valid tasks, measurement reliability, perceptual/acoustic alignment, language effects.
- **Patients/caregivers:** burden, privacy expectations, accessibility, meaningful output; avoid requests for identifying health records.
- **Rehabilitation:** repeated measurement and workflow fit without unsupported progression claims.

## Close

Ask what was missed, what evidence should be reviewed, and who else should be consulted. Record date, role category, setting type, consent status, and de-identified notes in an approved system. Keep quotations anonymous unless the participant explicitly agrees to attribution.
