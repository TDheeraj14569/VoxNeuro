# Solution rationale

**Status: DESIGN ASSUMPTION informed by technical literature; not a proven clinical solution.**

Voice is a plausible research signal because PD can involve hypokinetic dysarthria, changes in loudness and prosody, and phonatory irregularity. A browser-recorded sustained vowel offers a constrained task and inexpensive software prototype. That rationale supports studying repeatable acoustic measurement; it does not establish disease specificity, screening benefit, device equivalence, or improved outcomes.

The prototype therefore prioritizes: quality rejection, transparent signal processing, a versioned interpretable feature set, participant-grouped evaluation, a clear experimental label, and local-only operation. The approved new-recording prediction gate remains closed until recording-domain compatibility is demonstrated. A separate exploratory candidate may be shown only from fresh local evidence after input checks pass, and is explicitly not domain validated or clinically useful. The approach should be reconsidered if primary research indicates the task is burdensome, irrelevant to referral, or inequitable.
