# India regulatory preparation review

**Research snapshot: 2026-11-05. Not legal advice and not a regulatory classification.**

The product hypothesis includes voice-based Parkinson's screening/monitoring support. If software is intended to inform screening, diagnosis, monitoring, or clinical management, its status under India's Medical Devices Rules, 2017 and current CDSCO medical-device-software guidance must be determined with qualified regulatory counsel and CDSCO. Do not assume software-only means exempt and do not assign a class from this desk review.

CDSCO has published a 2026 “Guidance document on Medical Device Software under MDR-2017” (Doc. No. CDSCO/MD/GD/MDSW/01/2026). It discusses intended use, significance of information, risk-based classification, clinical evidence, privacy, traceability, AI risk, software lifecycle, cybersecurity, and post-market monitoring. Check the current CDSCO risk-classification list and seek the classification route from the Central Licensing Authority if the intended software is not listed. [Official CDSCO guidance](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf), [CDSCO medical devices portal](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Medical-Device-Diagnostics/Medical-Device-Diagnostics/), [Medical Devices Rules portal](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Acts-and-rules/Medical-Devices-Rules).

## Gap checklist

- Confirm intended use and whether claims constitute medical-device software.
- Obtain a written risk-classification view and identify licensing/manufacturing/clinical-investigation steps.
- Plan QMS and lifecycle evidence, traceability, verification/validation, cybersecurity, human factors, risk management, clinical evaluation, change control, vigilance, and post-market monitoring as applicable.
- Determine ethics/clinical investigation requirements before collecting patient data or conducting a pilot.
- Review current privacy rules, consent, data-controller/processor roles, retention, access, breach response, and cross-border issues with Indian counsel.
- No CDSCO engagement, authorization, certification, clinical study, or regulatory opinion has been obtained for VoxNeuro.
