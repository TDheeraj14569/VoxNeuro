Known Limitations & Bias Disclosures

  • UAMS has only 78 valid participants and severe age confounding (Age-only AUC/BA exceeds voice-only).
  • UCI #470 contains pre-extracted predictors without raw WAV audio or age.
  • External raw-audio cohorts (NeuroVoz, PC-GITA, Italian IPVS) remain unavailable without signed DUA + data.
  • Live microphone / ad-hoc uploads remain fail-closed unless explicit domain approval exists.
